      Edit config as JSON ?
      parent.shape
      "earth_default" (no other inputs) or "sphere_uniform" with radius_m, density_kg_m3 and rotation: spin_period_s, pole_ra_deg, pole_dec_deg, prime_meridian_deg.
      start_epoch
      "YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss.sss SCALE", SCALE one of UTC, TAI, TT, TDB, GPS.
      duration or end_epoch
      Seconds, or an ISO 8601 duration such as "P1DT6H". Or an end epoch in the start_epoch format. Not both.
      integrator
      {"type": "rk4", "time_step_s": 10}
      output_step_s
      Spacing of rows in plan.csv. Independent of the integration step.
      satellites
      count (1 to 100), mass_kg (up to 10000), names (list) or name_prefix, and orbits: one entry per satellite.
      orbits[ ]
      {"type": "keplerian", "a_m", "e", "i_deg", "raan_deg", "argp_deg", "ta_deg"} (osculating, inertial frame) or {"type": "cartesian", "r_m": [x, y, z], "v_m_s": [vx, vy, vz]}.
      earth_orientation.dut1_s
      Optional UT1 minus UTC in seconds for Earth. Default 0.
      force_model
      {"drag": true, "srp": true} turns on atmospheric drag and/or solar radiation pressure for Earth ("solar_drag" is accepted as another name for "srp"). Needs satellites.area_m2; satellites.cd defaults to 2.2, satellites.cr defaults to 1.8. Each orbit entry may override mass_kg, cd, cr, area_m2.
      satellites.pattern
      Use in place of orbits. {"type": "walker", "a_m", "i_deg", "t", "p", "f"} or {"type": "train", "base": {Keplerian orbit}, "offset": {"along_track_m" | "ta_deg" | "raan_deg"}} with count.
      orbits[ ] from a TLE
      {"type": "tle", "line1": "1 ...", "line2": "2 ..."}. SGP4 gives the state at start_epoch; the simulator's own force model takes it from there.
      maneuvers[ ]
      {"sat": name, "t_s" or "epoch", "dv_m_s": [a, b, c], "frame": "lvlh" | "inertial"}. For lvlh the three numbers are radial, along-track, cross-track.
